# KukGit Business Model

## Commercial thesis

KukGit can manage repositories for external users and can become an independent SaaS business. Revenue should not depend only on charging for repository storage. The higher-value products are AI, automation, security, enterprise governance and managed infrastructure.

## Proposed plans

### Free

- public and private repositories
- small team allowance
- basic issues and pull requests
- limited storage
- limited monthly automation and AI credits
- community support

Purpose: adoption and migration.

### Pro

For individual developers and freelancers:

- higher private storage
- more AI reviews
- advanced repository insights
- private packages
- priority build capacity

### Team

For startups and agencies:

- organization roles and teams
- branch protection
- higher CI and AI quotas
- shared secrets and environments
- audit history
- email support

### Business

- SSO
- advanced security scanning
- policy controls
- longer audit retention
- premium runners
- invoice billing
- support SLA

### Enterprise

- dedicated deployment or self-hosted
- SCIM/SAML
- data residency
- customer-managed encryption keys
- legal hold and compliance exports
- 24×7 support
- implementation and migration services

## Usage revenue

- CI runner minutes
- AI tokens/credits
- storage and egress
- artifact and package retention
- premium deployment environments
- security scanning seats or repositories

## Services revenue

- migration from GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket
- enterprise setup and training
- self-hosted implementation
- custom integrations
- managed DevOps and security

## Marketplace revenue

Kuklabs can charge a percentage on paid apps, workflow templates, AI agents and deployment integrations.

## Differentiation

KukGit should not sell “another place to store Git.” It should sell:

- fewer engineering tools
- faster review and delivery
- visible technical debt
- AI agents with controlled repository access
- integrated KukTask, KukMeet, KukChat and KukBook workflows
- simpler enterprise deployment for India, GCC and global SMBs

## Cost controls

- free-plan storage and egress quotas
- AI credit limits and model routing
- shared runners with queue limits
- automatic artifact retention
- cold storage for inactive repositories
- abuse prevention for public CI and package hosting
